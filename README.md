# ClauseFinder
Terms of Service (ToS) and Privacy Policy documents are often lengthy and difficult for users to review. Important provisions, such as mandatory arbitration, automatic subscription renewal, and third-party data sharing, can be difficult to locate. ClauseFinder aims to help users quickly identify the passages most relevant to these concerns.
